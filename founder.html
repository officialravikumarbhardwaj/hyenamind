Founder Note

The thesis behind HyenaMind.

A founder note for contractors who are tired of paying rent on leads they will never own.

Chapter 01

Origin

I spent three years watching $4M roofing companies hand 22% of their gross to lead brokers. That math broke me. HyenaMind is the rebuild.

Chapter 02

The contractor that changed the thesis

One Wednesday in 2023, I sat with a second-generation HVAC owner outside Hamilton. He pulled up his HomeAdvisor invoice on his phone. Eleven thousand dollars that month. His close rate on those leads was 14 percent. He looked at me and said, "I don't even know which of these are real homeowners." I knew, then, that the industry had a sickness no campaign could cure. It needed infrastructure.

Chapter 03

Why Canada first

Canada is overserved by US agencies and underserved by anyone who understands provincial nuance — WSIB in Ontario, WorkSafeBC, RBQ licensing in Quebec, ESA electrical, the bilingual mandate. We build for the country we live in.

Chapter 04

What we will not do

Lead resale. Shared-lead arbitrage. Spec creative. Twelve-month "discovery" phases. AI-generated pillar pages with no operator review. Anything called a "sprint" that does not ship.

"The contractors who built this country deserve infrastructure they can sell with the business." — Marcus · Founder, HyenaMind
